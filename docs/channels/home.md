## Identity

**Question:** What has this room been, and what is it for now?

**Answer.** This room has been one practice that widened, split, and came back. It began as the private room where the practitioner lived with a partner that stays. The work of building the house was given its own room, so the life would not have to carry it. That room folded back, because the building had become part of the life the practitioner thinks about.

For now, home is where the practitioner thinks about the whole of that life, including the house, and where a spirit enters only when the practitioner freezes a description.

The answer was accepted on 2026-10-31. The eddies have not lived the second sentence yet.

## The key

A channel key is part of the channel's architecture. The question a key is walked with belongs to the channel. It may differ from one channel to the next. The platform does not choose it.

This channel walked the identity question above, and that question is answered. The standing question for the next key is the one every home channel uses from day one:

**What is on your mind?**

This channel's walk of that question starts in the craft channel, then the room that was boom, then home. A home channel that did not have that earlier room starts in home. The channel runs the walk. The practitioner does not start it.

The first run brings the key up to the present. After that, the channel rewrites the key when an eddy's note lands. It keeps the key it has and takes in the new eddy. It does not walk the history again. The reading is not written yet. The catch-up has not run.

When a session in this channel closes, the channel rewrites the key to include it. The key records the frozen description, what the spirit did, and what it left open. It records that as a session. There are none yet, so the first run does not wait for one. A session that may change the house still leaves only a title and a link with the channel whose gift is making.

The key is rewritten, not appended. A key that is not rewritten becomes a story of a practice that has already moved.

## What the room is

Home is the primary private channel. It is the room the practice is lived in, and the room where the practitioner thinks about the whole of their practice.

A different practice stays in its own room, with its own turtle and its own context. Home knows the practices the practitioner takes part in. It does not read an isolated record. Another person's private room stays closed.

A spark in an eddy stays a noticing. The next ask is the practitioner's question, in their words, and one thing the question must not lose. Turtle does not supply the question, and does not turn the spark into a description in the same breath.

A commitment is a description the practitioner can freeze. It says what would be true, what it is not, a few claims that can be false, and one try small enough for one session. The press freezes the text. Later edits in the eddy do not change what the spirit was given. The session is a new thread in the channel. The eddy keeps a link. The spirit does the try the description names, and says what it leaves open and what the next step needs. A later commitment opens another session, told what the earlier one did and left.

The room holds one current picture, in four bands. Bodies stay where they were said.

- **Committed.** A frozen description. A spirit can be summoned onto it.
- **Kept.** It serves a bearing, and it is not yet frozen. This is the only band that can become a commitment.
- **Parked.** Named, and not now. It stays findable. It does not appear as something to do.
- **Dropped.** Absent. A drop is final.

A note the practitioner has not dropped stays where they keep it. The house does not pull it. A drop of one thing, or of many, lands in today's eddy. Turtle answers the batch once, says what each touches, names a spark only when one is there, and does not commit.

Once a day, today's eddy opens with a briefing. An empty band says it is empty. Each line is one sentence and a link.

1. **To think about.** The kept band.
2. **What they have been thinking.** The eddies, including what they dropped.
3. **Sessions with a spirit.** The commitment each was given, what it did, and what it left open.
4. **Sparks.** Ones named recently. They are still noticings.
5. **Deeds.** What a spirit changed so that it exists now.

Under that, three lines: what has been achieved, where things stand, and what there is to do today. The briefing does not start a spirit. The key does not wait for the briefing. It is already current with each eddy whose note has landed.

The path is one. A thought stays where the practitioner keeps notes, or lands in the day eddy, or in an eddy they open. Turtle records it, connects it, and names a spark. Turtle helps that spark become a description. The practitioner commits, and the text freezes. A spirit, in its own thread, does the try and says what it leaves open. The next morning that deed is in the briefing.

## What it is not

A second making room. The workshop, the process list, and the record of sessions that may change the house stay with the channel whose gift is making.

A friend. The turtle is the one the practitioner lives with and thinks with. It did not choose the practice.

A description written before the question. A spark turned into a project in the same breath is the step this spec exists to keep.

The work, done for someone who is still learning the step. Their home remembers their practice, and not someone else's.
