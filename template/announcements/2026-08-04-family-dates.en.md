# Turtle now keeps important dates

You can now trust Turtle with dates that matter — birthdays, school events, deadlines, appointments.

## How it works

Just mention it in conversation: *"the kita fest is on 12.9."* (day.month) — Turtle offers: **Keep this date?** The confirmation spells the date out in words — *Saturday, September 12* — so nothing gets mixed up. One click, done.

After that, there is **nothing** to maintain. No calendar to tend, no list to keep. Turtle surfaces it in time — a week before and on the day, or however you ask (*"remind me two weeks before so I can get a gift"*).

For those who prefer typed commands: `!date <when> <what>` captures directly, `!dates` lists what's coming up.

## What Turtle does not do

A reminder is support, not a task assignment. Turtle lets you know — what you do with it stays yours.

Dates from a shared space surface in that space. Dates from your own river stay in your river.
